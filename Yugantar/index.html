Ancient India


The ancient period of India spans from the Indus Valley Civilization (c. 3300 BCE) to the end of the Gupta Empire (c.1206 CE). Tis era saw the rise of powerful empires, the development of religion, philosophy, science, art, and the foundation of Indian Culture


Major Empires and Dynasties of Ancient India


Indus Valley Civilization

c.3300 - 1300 BCE

One of the world's earliest urban civilizations, known
for its advanced planning, drainage system and trade network

Explore

Maurya Empire

322 - 185 BCE

Founded by Chandragupta Maurya, expanded by Ashoka the
Great. Known for a strong administration, Buddhism and a unified India

Explore

Gupta Empire

320 - 550 CE

Often called the Golden Age of India. Saw progress
in science, mathematics, literature, art and culture

Explore

Chola Empire

c.850 - 1279 BCE

Known for it's naval power, architecture
(Brihadeeswarar Temple) and a rich culture heritage

Explore

Delhi Sultanate

1206 - 1526 BCE

Established by Qutb-ud-din Aibak, Introduced Islamic rule
in northern India and laid the foundation for the Mughal Empire
